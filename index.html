How to use this Playbook

The strip above reads HPN's ATIS and checks the runways in use against the SOP's tower rules. The HPN SOP has no runway selection table: the configuration comes from the ATIS, and N90 provides the TRACON services.

Rules on every departure

  • Assign the HPN# departure (say "WESTCHESTER (number)") to all aircraft.
  • PROP# departure: runway 16 only, and only when NY TRACON requests it. Never issue it otherwise.
  • Initial altitude 3,000 ft. HPN SIDs have no published altitude, so say "MAINTAIN 3,000."
  • Releases are automatic unless N90 asks for individual releases.
  • Standard departure separation: anticipated radar separation.

Clearance Delivery

Every IFR departure gets the HPN# departure and "maintain 3,000." The only alternative is PROP#, which NY TRACON must ask for.

Ground Control

Sequence by alternating gate, then alternating exit fix, then performance. Never approve an intersection departure from taxiway Hotel.

Departure sequencing

  1. By alternating gate.
  2. If not that, by alternating exit fix.
  3. If not that, by aircraft performance, fastest to slowest.

Intersection departures

Taxiway Hotel sits at the intersection of runways 11/29 and 16/34, which confuses pilots asking for an intersection departure. Intersection departures from Hotel, on any runway, may not be approved.

Aircraft exiting the runway

Local turns arrivals onto a taxiway parallel to the landing runway, so they keep taxiing away from the exit, and hands them to Ground once they're taxiing away. Local and Ground coordinate the taxiway flow as needed.

Local Control

Releases are automatic, separation is anticipated radar separation, and the tower never radar identifies IFR departures.

Airspace and radar

  • The tower owns below 3,000 ft within 4 nm of the airport, and below 3,000 ft out to 6 nm along the runway 16/34 extended centerline, 1.5 nm either side.
  • Westchester ATCT is Limited Radar Approach Control certified.
  • Don't radar identify departing IFR aircraft. Switch them to Departure once clear of traffic, after seeing them turn toward the assigned climb, heading or visual reporting point.
  • The tower may radar identify VFR aircraft when needed for identification and separation or sequencing in the Class D.

Intersecting runways: runway 29 arrivals with 16/34 in use

The runway 29 displaced threshold is close to the edge of 16/34. A runway 29 arrival may not cross the original runway 29 threshold until:

  • the aircraft landing 16/34 has passed the runway 29 intersection, or has finished its landing roll and will hold short of it, or
  • the aircraft departing 16/34 has passed the runway 29 intersection, or is airborne and turning to avoid conflict.

In all other cases apply JO 7110.65 and use the runway 29 displaced threshold as the separation point.

Daylight VFR only, and the LAHSO runway's landing distance must be dry (no visible moisture). The SOP's weather minimum reads "ceiling 100' and visibility 3 miles".

VFR pattern work

Pattern work on runway 11/29 makes its downwind over the runway 16 or 34 numbers, whichever matches the configuration, to stay clear of IFR arrivals.

MAKE LEFT CLOSED TRAFFIC, MAKE YOUR DOWNWIND OVER THE RUNWAY 34 NUMBERS, RUNWAY 29, CLEARED FOR TAKEOFF.
